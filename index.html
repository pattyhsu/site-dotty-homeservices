How we buy

Three steps, no showings.

You don't have to fix, clean or stage anything. We buy the house in the condition it's in.

Tell us about the house

Send the address and a few details, or have your agent send it. We look at the numbers the same day.

Get a written offer

We walk the property and send a purchase agreement with a cash price. There are no hidden fees or repair credits.

Close through escrow

A neutral escrow and title company handles the funds. You choose the closing date, and you can leave behind anything you don't want.

Not sure a cash sale is right for you? Ask us. If your house doesn't need much work and listing it would net you more, we'll tell you honestly.
